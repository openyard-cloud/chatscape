Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Chatscape is a browser extension that draws animated wallpapers behind the chat on claude.ai and chatgpt.com. It is built so that nothing about you or your conversations leaves your browser.

What Chatscape collects

Nothing. Chatscape has no servers, sends no network requests, and contains no analytics, tracking or remote code.

What it stores, and where

Your settings only: the chosen scene, the Dim, Glass and Speed values, and whether Chatscape is on. They are saved with chrome.storage.sync and, as a fallback on this device, chrome.storage.local, so your browser may sync them across your own signed-in Chrome profiles. Chatscape never sees or transmits them anywhere else.

What it reads on the page

  • Computed background colours and sizes of page elements, to make the right panels see-through. It does not read their text.
  • The text in the message box only at the moment you press Enter, and only to check whether it is exactly /chatscape. Nothing is stored or sent.

Permissions

  • storage: to remember your settings.
  • Access to https://claude.ai/* and https://chatgpt.com/*: the only sites where Chatscape runs.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and in the source repository, where you can also open an issue with any question.

Chatscape is independent open-source software and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic or OpenAI.
