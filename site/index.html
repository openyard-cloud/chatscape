Made to stay out of the way.

A wallpaper should be pleasant, not loud. Every control is one click away.

Open it three ways

Click the toolbar button, press Alt+Shift+T, or type /chatscape in the chat box and press Enter. The message is not sent.

Dim and Glass

Dim lays a veil in the page's own colour over the scene. Glass sets how see-through the sidebar and composer are. Text stays easy to read.

Private by design

One permission (storage), runs only on claude.ai and chatgpt.com, no network requests, no analytics. It never reads your messages.

Light on your laptop

Up to 30 frames per second, pauses when the tab is hidden, and a still frame when your system asks for reduced motion.

Off means off

Turn off restores the page exactly as it was. Your choice syncs across your Chrome profiles.

Add your own scene

A scene is a few lines of data: gradients, stars, waves, sprites. No drawing code. Send a pull request and it ships to everyone.

Install in a minute.

The Chrome Web Store listing is on its way. Until then, install it by hand:

Download

Download .zip

Unzip it

Double-click the file. You get a folder named chatscape.

Load it

Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder.

Open Claude or ChatGPT

Reload claude.ai or chatgpt.com. Cloud Drift appears behind your chat. Press Alt+Shift+T to change it.
