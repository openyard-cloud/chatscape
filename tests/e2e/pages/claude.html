Weekend plans
Can you plan a calm weekend in Da Lat for two?

Here is a slow weekend that leaves room to rest. Arrive Friday evening and walk around Xuan Huong lake while the market opens. On Saturday, take the early cable car to Truc Lam and have lunch near the flower gardens, then spend the afternoon in a café with a view of the pine hills. Sunday is for the night market leftovers you missed, a long breakfast, and the drive back before the evening traffic.
